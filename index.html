About us

A design office that thinks like the workshop

Damodar Engineering Design is a Sharjah-based engineering design practice serving fabricators, EPC contractors and equipment OEMs across the UAE, the GCC and India.

We were founded on a simple idea: the best drawings are written for the people who build from them. Every model we create and every sheet we issue is checked for fit-up, weld access, lifting and transport before it reaches your shop floor, so your team spends its time building rather than chasing clarifications.

Our work covers the full design journey, from concept layouts and 3D models to GA, fabrication and assembly drawings, with support through fabrication queries, FAT and SAT. We work to the codes your project specifies, in your title block and your formats, as a natural extension of your own engineering team.

Execution pipeline

From enquiry to issued drawings

Seven stages that keep revisions off the shop floor.

  1. Enquiry & scope

    Share specs, P&ID and battery limits. Agree deliverables and timeline.

  2. Design basis

    Fix codes, materials, design pressures and authority requirements.

  3. Sizing & vendors

    Footprint sizing, datasheet checks and nozzle orientation freeze.

  4. 3D design & piping

    Frame, piping routing, valve access and CoG checks in SolidWorks.

  5. GA, drawings & BOM

    GA, isometrics, weld details, cut lists and multi-level BOMs.

  6. Fabrication support

    Answers to shop-floor queries, concessions and as-built markups.

  7. FAT / SAT & handover

    Test protocols, inspection sign-off and native CAD handover.
